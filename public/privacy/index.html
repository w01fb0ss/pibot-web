隐私政策Privacy Policy

这份政策说明 Pi Bot 桌面应用、手机应用和连接插件如何处理数据。网站本身不需要你的账号，也不读取 Gmail。This policy explains how Pi Bot desktop and mobile apps and connected plugins handle data. The website does not need your account and does not read Gmail.

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 16 日Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. 你使用的是什么服务

Pi Bot 是一个以本地电脑为大脑的桌面应用。对话、Bot 配置、记忆、连接设置和操作记录默认保存在你的设备上。手机和执行端通过端到端加密连接；官方中继只转发加密数据。

2. Pi Bot 网站收集什么

本网站不要求登录，不放广告，不出售个人信息，也不把聊天、文件、邮件正文、邮件附件或模型密钥上传到网站。官网下载按钮的点击、应用的更新检查和官方中继只保留按日期、版本、平台或设备类型汇总的统计，不用于识别个人。

3. 模型和连接服务商

你选择云端模型、Gmail 或其他连接时，请求中的必要内容会发送给对应服务商。服务商如何处理这些内容由它自己的政策和条款决定。使用本地模型时，模型请求可以留在本机。你可以在应用的权限、连接和隐私设置中查看并关闭相应能力。

4. Gmail OAuth

Gmail 插件申请当前已启用工具所需的最小 Gmail 权限，并申请 Google 账号的已验证邮箱地址以识别你连接的账号。授权在系统浏览器中由你确认。OAuth 授权期间，Pi Bot 授权服务会暂时接收 Google 的授权码并向 Google 换取令牌，再把一次性加密票据返回你的本机；授权码、访问令牌和刷新令牌不会写入该服务的持久存储。刷新令牌随后加密保存在这台电脑上、该插件安装专用的凭据空间；更新访问令牌时，本地会把刷新令牌通过 HTTPS 交给授权服务转向 Google 交换，授权服务不保存这些令牌。访问令牌只在请求内存中使用，不写入日志、文件、聊天或网站。该服务不会接收或保存你的 Gmail 邮件正文、附件、联系人或密码。对 Google 用户数据的使用遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy 的 Limited Use 要求。

5. 你的控制权

  • 你可以在 Google 账号的第三方连接页面撤销 Gmail 授权，也可以在 Pi Bot 连接页断开并删除本地凭据。
  • 你可以删除本地数据目录，或使用应用提供的删除入口清理 Bot、连接和历史。
  • 你可以关闭连接、模型、手机照片和其他可选能力；关闭后不会继续为这些能力发起请求。

6. 安全与保留

我们按应用所需的时间保留本地数据；网站不保存应用内容。密钥和 OAuth 令牌使用操作系统提供的安全存储和安装级隔离。没有任何网络传输或存储方式可以保证绝对安全，请使用设备锁、系统更新和可信账号。

7. 政策变化和联系

我们会在本页更新日期并发布变更。若你对隐私有疑问，请通过 Pi Bot 网站项目反馈页联系维护者；不要在公开反馈中提交邮件、令牌或其他个人信息。

1. The service

Pi Bot is a desktop app whose brain runs on your computer. Conversations, Bot settings, memories, connection settings, and activity records are stored on your devices by default. Phones and runners use end-to-end encrypted connections; the official relay only forwards encrypted data.

2. What the Pi Bot website collects

This website does not require an account, serve ads, sell personal information, or upload chats, files, email bodies, email attachments, or model keys. Website download clicks, app update checks, and the official relay keep only aggregate counts by date, version, platform, or device type, without identifying you.

3. Models and connected services

When you choose a cloud model, Gmail, or another connection, the necessary request content is sent to that service provider. The provider's own policy and terms govern its handling. With a local model, model requests can stay on your computer. You can review and disable these capabilities in the app's permissions, connections, and privacy settings.

4. Gmail OAuth

The Gmail plugin requests the minimum Gmail scopes required by the tools you have enabled, plus your verified Google account email address to identify the connected account. You approve authorization in your system browser. During authorization, Pi Bot's authorization service temporarily receives Google's authorization code, exchanges it with Google, and returns a one-time encrypted ticket to this device; authorization codes, access tokens, and refresh tokens are not written to that service's persistent storage. The refresh token is then encrypted in an installation-scoped credential store on this computer. To renew access, the local app sends the refresh token over HTTPS to the authorization service for exchange with Google; the service does not retain these tokens. access tokens are used only in memory for a request and are never written to logs, files, chats, or the website. The service does not receive or store your Gmail message bodies, attachments, contacts, or password. Use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Your choices

  • You can revoke Gmail access from your Google Account's third-party connections page, then disconnect the plugin and delete its local credentials in Pi Bot.
  • You can delete the local data directory or use the app's deletion controls to remove Bots, connections, and history.
  • You can turn off a connection, model, phone photo access, or another optional capability; the app will not make requests for a disabled capability.

6. Security and retention

Local data is retained for as long as the app needs it; the website does not store app content. Keys and OAuth tokens use operating-system secure storage and installation-scoped isolation. No network or storage method is absolutely secure, so use device locks, system updates, and trusted accounts.

7. Changes and contact

We update the date on this page when this policy changes. For privacy questions, contact the maintainers through the Pi Bot website issue tracker; do not include email content, tokens, or other personal information in a public issue.
