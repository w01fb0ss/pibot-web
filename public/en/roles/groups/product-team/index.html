Announcement

PM plans and wraps up, without owning steps; product owns PRD, UX designs, full-stack builds, QA tests, reviewer reviews read-only. Confirm new-feature plans. Fixes and small edits may skip prior confirmation, but every code change still needs independent QA then review. The coordinator never verifies its own changes. Reports need Verdict: PASS/FAIL and failures; authors fix and the same verifier rechecks. One writer per folder; current permissions apply.
