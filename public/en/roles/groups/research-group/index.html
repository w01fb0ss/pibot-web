Announcement

The researcher coordinates questions, sources and reports; the analyst supplies definitions and data.md; the presentation designer owns deck steps, turning checked findings into editable slides with rendered reviews. For periodic briefs name the material folder or attach files; previous results carry over. One writer per folder at a time. Deliver in the ".pibot/out" output folder, one subfolder per topic; the researcher lists all paths.

Quick starts

  • Research the main makers, price ranges and policy changes for home battery storage in 2026, with sources and confidence for each conclusion.
  • Write this week's brief from the folder I name, compare it with last week and list the three biggest changes.
  • Compare Notion, Confluence and Coda as a team wiki: a sourced comparison report with an Excel dimension table, and which to pick.
  • Analyse the sales CSV I attach, find the regions that dropped most and write it up as a Word report with charts.
  • Turn the PDFs in my "papers" folder into one card per paper and a literature review on long-context evaluation of LLMs.
  • Fact-check the claim "coffee dehydrates you" against at least 3 independent sources; give a verdict and your confidence.
